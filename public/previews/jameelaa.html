Not just a collection of screens.

Jameelaa is a live service-booking product with multiple moving parts: booking flows, payment states, role-specific behavior, notifications and the operational systems that keep those parts consistent.

01 — Define the contracts.

The API needs to support real clients, not only the newest build. That means backward-compatible changes, careful versioning and reliable responses.

02 — Treat workflows as systems.

Bookings and payments depend on state transitions, asynchronous events, background jobs and recovery paths. Laravel, PostgreSQL, Redis and queues are part of that engineering work.

03 — Make shipping repeatable.

Automated checks, containerized environments, controlled rollout and rollback plans help make releases predictable.

04 — Own it after launch.

Observability, security and incident readiness are part of the job. The goal is to keep improving a real product safely.

My role: Full-stack engineering, backend architecture, infrastructure, delivery and operations. Individual mobile implementation work is credited separately.
